Balancing the Terran units

An analysis of the 9 unit sheets checked against the current server, a proposed set of numbers for every weapon and ability, and the questions still open. None of this touches code: if the proposal is approved, it will be carried over to the sheets.

APPer individual, for movement and attacks
LevelsWeapons and abilities stack
ClassesLight / Medium / Heavy + air domain
SpeedAP cost per tile and individual

Core idea. Every armor class has weapons that deal full damage to it and weapons that barely scratch it (30%). That way the question every turn is always the same: “what's in front of me and which weapon breaks it?”. The numbers below are tuned so that the right unit against its target is 2–3 times more efficient per AP than the wrong one.

Issues in the current sheets

The most important things found when reading the 9 sheets together, with each one's numbers:

  • Rifle range. It reaches 8 tiles (Missile launcher 6, Machine gun 5, Raptor's Burst 4). It makes sense for it to be the longest-range infantry weapon thanks to the telescopic sight, but 8 exceeds the vision of almost every unit.
  • The Mech barely attacks. Its only ground weapon can be used once every 3 turns (Flamethrower at G2, Freezergun at G3). Two turns out of three, a heavy unit costing 300 E + 50 steel cannot fire. On top of that the Flamethrower deals 20, the same as a Military's Machine gun.
  • Damage on different scales. “× individual”, “per unit”, “per burst”, “per mine” and “50 damage” with no unit. The Raptor's Burst (250 for 3 AP, 83 per AP) is 8 times more efficient than the Rifle (10 per AP).
  • With AP per individual, some costs are unaffordable. Anti-air costs 5 AP per individual: a troop of 8 Mechs would spend 40 AP (twice what a Government 2 gives today). Reflector shield costs 250 E per individual: 2000 E for 8 Mechs.
  • The Raptor carries 20 and the APC 10 (15 with Capacity+). The transport vehicle loses its reason to exist next to the fighter, which also flies and attacks.
  • The Raptor is an air unit but is “Blocked by: Water, Mountain”, and it is Heavy class, just like the Mech.
  • Areas with no center tile. 2x2 (Flamethrower, Gas, EMP) and 4x4 (Quantum bomb) have no center. The Bomb says “100% on the center tile, −25% per tile”, which only works with odd-sized areas.
  • Quantum bomb cost: “500 damage”. It is probably the damage, not the cost.
  • Inconsistent profiles. The Rifle has 2 percentages (the Medium ones are missing). Mines are AntiLight, but the description says “against every kind of unit”.
  • Turbo: 200 fuel per use. You start with 100 and a Refinery produces 30 per worker per turn.
  • Missing durations: Camouflage, Stim, Reflector shield, Turbo.
  • Two abilities do the same thing: the Droid's Shield+ (+50% shields) and the Scientist's Shield Aura (+25%). The auras say “3x3 target area”, but a passive is not cast on any spot.
  • Strength+ and Strength++ add +50 health: a G3 Military would have 100, the same health as the current Mech.
  • The APC has the same health and shield as a Military (50 / 50) despite being an armored vehicle.
  • Speed and Armor are blank on every sheet. Explorer, Droid and Scientist have no stats.

Classes: armor, domain and nature

Today the server only distinguishes Light and Heavy, and every building counts as Heavy. The sheets add Medium and air units, but mix everything into a single label. The proposal is three independent axes, each with a clear job:

AxisValuesWhat it decides
ArmorLight · Medium · Heavy · StructureThe % of damage it takes depending on the weapon's profile (table below).
DomainGround · AirWhether a weapon can reach it. Ground weapons don't hit air units; Air weapons only hit air units; Ground-to-Air hits both. Air units ignore water and mountains.
Nature (new)Organic · MechanicalIt only decides who Paralyzing gas affects (organic: people). EMP affects everything with a shield, units and buildings.

Why the Raptor becomes Medium. If it is Heavy, it shares its counter with the Mech and the Missile launcher kills both equally. As a Medium air unit it fits as a “fast vehicle”: anti-air weapons destroy it (the Mech's Anti-air, Air Defense) and the Missile launcher only does 60% to it. The Mech remains the only Heavy: slow, expensive and very tough, the natural target of the Missile launcher and the Burst.

Why Structure. Separating buildings lets anti-personnel weapons (Rifle, Flamethrower, Turret) do very little to a building (25%). That way a siege needs siege weapons (Missile launcher, Burst, Bomb) instead of piling up Militaries.

Quick read: what breaks what

  • Infantry (Light ground): the Mech's Flamethrower, Rifle, Turret, Bite, Mines and Bomb.
  • APC (Medium ground): Laser machine gun (Military G3), Freezergun, Mines, Bomb.
  • Raptor (Medium air): the Mech's Anti-air and Air Defense; the Missile launcher does 60%.
  • Mech (Heavy): Missile launcher and Burst. Anti-personnel weapons barely hurt it.
  • Buildings: Missile launcher, Burst and, at half strength, the Bomb.
  • Droid (Light air): only anti-air weapons can hit it, at 30–60%. It is hard to kill on purpose, because it doesn't attack and its value lies in the portal.

Rules

  • From the next round. A unit gives AP starting the turn after it is created: producing and spending in the same turn doesn't pay off.
  • Paralyzed or frozen, it gives no AP. Paralyzing gas and Freezergun also cut the rival's AP that turn.
  • One troop per turn at each military building, of the level's maximum size (e.g. 5 militaries at G1).
  • No artificial caps. The military cap, upkeep and resource costs already limit how many units (and therefore AP) you have. If you lose troops, you lose the AP that moved them.

Speed: AP per tile and individual

The cost of moving a troop is individuals × tiles × speed, rounded up at the end of the order. There is no cap on tiles per turn: the limit is AP and, for the Military and the Scientist, fatigue (below). Modifiers divide or multiply that cost: +25% speed means dividing by 1.25 and “Slows by 50%” doubles it.

Fatigue: Military and Scientist (v10)

Human units that walk get tired; machines don't. Approved

  • +1 fatigue per tile, straight or diagonal, from 0 to 10. The first 10 tiles of each turn cost the normal amount.
  • At 10, the troop stops and yields its AP: attacks, construction and economic orders are resolved first, even if they come later in the queue.
  • At the end of the turn it keeps walking tired with whatever AP are left. Tired tile number k costs the normal cost × (1 + k/2): ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5…
  • Each turn it resets to 0. When troops merge, the higher fatigue is kept; when they split, both halves inherit it. Riding inside an APC or a Raptor causes no fatigue.

The route is drawn in segments: solid as far as the troop gets without tiring (for machines, as far as the turn's AP reach), wavy where it walks tired and dashed for whatever it doesn't cover this turn. In unexplored fog the route goes in a straight line, since nobody knows what lies underneath either, and it is recalculated when an obstacle is discovered. Buildings can also be ordered blind in the fog (question 69).

Fuel: spent when moving

Fuel is no longer upkeep: it is only spent when moving and on some abilities (Flamethrower, Turbo). A parked unit consumes nothing. Approved The per-tile values come from spreading today's upkeep over the tiles the unit walks in a typical turn, so an army on the march spends the same as before and an idle one spends nothing.

The Refinery can be built from Government 2, at the same time Mechs and APCs become available; full (4 workers) it yields 120 per turn and moves 6 Mechs 3 tiles (54) with room to spare for the Flamethrower.

What is purchased

Each unit comes with its first weapon and its Government 1 abilities. The other weapons, the Government 2 and 3 active abilities and all passives are purchased once per player, with energy, at the building that produces the unit: it costs 3 AP, the upgrade is ready 2 turns later and it requires the matching Government level. The “++” passives require having bought the “+” one first.

Energy cap (v10)

Generators never run dry, so energy no longer grows in a straight line: it fills up like a tank. Each turn the gross production comes in multiplied by how much is left to fill it, and it never goes over the maximum, however many Generators you have. Spending speeds production up again. Approved

ΔE = P × (1 − E / Max), where P is the turn's gross production (Government + 75 per worker at each Generator) and E is the energy you already have.

Energy maximumAddsExample
Government level 1 / 2 / 32,000 / 4,000 / 6,000Tank base
Each finished Generator+1,500G2 + 2 Generators: 7,000 · G3 + 4 Generators: 12,000
  • More workers fill it sooner, not higher. With G2 and 2 Generators (cap 7,000), starting from 1,500 and spending nothing: with 1 worker per Generator you reach 6,000 around turn 27; with 4, around turn 12.
  • Only production is limited. What comes from the Bank or from refunds goes in whole. If you lose a Generator and end up above the new maximum, you don't lose energy: it just stops growing.
  • Upkeep still subtracts. With the tank almost full little comes in, so energy settles somewhat below the maximum.
  • The AI no longer takes energy loans. With the cap, the loan slowed production down and paying it back (1.5×) left it negative.

Result in the simulations (1v1, 6 maps, round 60): between 3,300 and 11,800 energy (previously 7,300 to 39,900), and every player still reaches Government 3 except one, who stays at level 2.

Vision and range

Vision belongs to the unit; range, to each weapon. A troop can fire at any target within its weapon's range that it sees itself or that any allied unit or building sees. Approved That way an Explorer can mark targets for long-range weapons. Defenses follow the same rule. Since weapons stack, the Military always carries the Rifle and its vision already accounts for the telescopic sight. Long-range weapons deal less damage; the hard-hitting ones (Flamethrower, Machine gun, Bomb) force you to get close.

Building health

Approved With the new weapons, the server's current health values fall short: 10 Militaries with Missile launchers destroy almost any building in a single order. The proposal distributes health according to what is lost if the building falls, measured in attack orders from three reference attackers (one order = one troop attacking for one turn). Shield = 25% of health, except for the Government (question 40).

Criterion. With anti-personnel weapons (Rifle) an important building takes 20 orders or more: sieging with them isn't worth it. With siege weapons, the Government holds for 3 orders at G1 and 7 at G3, factories about 4, Defenses 2–3 and the economy 1–2, so that raids remain possible.

Why these numbers

  • Government. Level 3 doesn't change (7 Missile launcher orders, several turns of siege). Levels 1 and 2 go up so that a player one level behind doesn't fall in a single turn: Government 1 against Missile launchers goes from 2 to 3 orders, and against Rifles from 14 to 22.
  • Training Center and Ground Factory. Losing them cuts production and blocks the Government upgrade. They go from 2 to 4 Missile launcher orders: destroying them requires committing an army, not a lone troop.
  • Launch Pad. The most expensive building (G3); a little more than the factories.
  • Ground Defense. With the Missile launcher at 6 it can be sieged without taking fire (question 51); with 800 health it takes 3 orders instead of 1, so it is still worth building. With Rifles, also at range 6, it would take 20.
  • Air Defense. Ground units are immune to it and destroy it easily, so it doesn't need as much health as the Ground one. The Raptor (Burst, range 4) takes its fire (range 6).
  • Economy. Generator, extractors, Farm and Refinery go up a little: a raid with siege weapons must be able to take them out in 1–2 orders. The Generator, somewhat more because of energy and the military cap.
  • Housing. No changes: it is cheap and rebuilt in one turn.
  • Repair. Its cost is a percentage of the building's cost, not of its health, so more health doesn't make repairs more expensive.

What would need to change on the server

Only to size the work; no code is touched until the sheets are final.

  • Current values are very different from the sheets. On the server the Military deals 50 (the sheet says 10), the Robot 200 with a “Laser machine gun” and the Ship 150 in a 3x3 area with an “Ion missile”. None of those weapons match the sheets.
  • Classes. Medium and Structure need to be added, along with the air domain, each weapon's mode and the organic/mechanical nature.
  • Several weapons per unit. Today each unit has a single attack. It needs weapons per level, stacking, selectable in the attack order, each with its own damage, profile, range, cost and cooldown.
  • Range. Today range equals vision. It would become a property of each weapon, and units could fire at whatever any ally sees (today only at what the unit itself sees).
  • AP. Today attacking costs a flat 3 AP per order and moving costs 1 AP per tile for the whole troop. It would become AP per individual with a speed per type, with income from the Government level and from each unit (Housing stops giving AP).
  • Shield per troop. Today a troop of 10 Militaries has a single shield of 50, and when it splits both halves keep the whole shield, so splitting gives away free shield. The proposal is a shield per individual.
  • Fuel. Today it is upkeep per turn (Robot 10, Vehicle 15, Ship 20). It would be spent per tile when moving, like the Constructor's energy (which the Explorer would also use).
  • Invisibility and detection. Energy cost per turn while it lasts, and passive detection within the vision of the Government and the G3 Explorer (Detect camouflaged passive); both also see mines.
  • Abilities and states. They don't exist: active abilities with cooldowns, passives and auras would need to be created, along with the burn, slowed, paralyzed, invisible and temporary shield states.
  • New mechanics: flight (today the Ship is blocked by water and mountains), transport, mines, portal and subsoil.
  • Area damage. Today only the Ship does area damage (3x3, same damage on every tile). The size would need to be configurable, with falloff by distance.
  • Air Defense. Today it doesn't shoot; only the Ground Defense has automatic fire.
  • Units and buildings. Rename Robot → Mech, Vehicle → APC and Ship → Raptor. Create the Explorer, Droid and Scientist. The Aircraft Factory becomes the Launch Pad and requires Government 3 (2 today).
  • AI and client. The AI has to learn to choose weapons and abilities while keeping the simulations fast. The client needs an interface to choose a weapon or ability, show states and compute AP per individual in the previews.
