AXIOM WARS CHANGE HISTORY
(The version is the date of the latest change to the server or the client.)

v2026.11.10 alpha
    AI: it thinks 2.8-3.2 times faster without changing its decisions, so matches with many troops run smoother
    Portals: the rays are drawn as a single path per layer (+21 % FPS with a portal network) and are no longer seen in pieces

v2026.11.09 alpha
    Balance: the Militar loses damage (Government 2: -33%, Government 3: -40%) and moves to 56 life and 29 shield, with troops of 4/6/7; the Explorador gets 88 life, Bite +22% (Government 2) and +40% (Government 3), Dentellada +40%, and troops of 6/8; the Meca's shield goes up to 89; the APC drops to 117 life but its Turret and Cannon hit +40% at Government 3
    Explorador: Move buried replaces Speed+ (buried it moves at half speed, spends double Energy, passes under other troops and attacks by surfacing); the AI uses it for ambushes and buys combat Exploradores
    Portals: troops overlap on the footprint of a portal in use and land in a wider radius
    Maps: with starting Government 2 or 3 the starting base always fits (Factory, puddles and steel deposit), and tame fauna no longer takes the spot
    Portals: a group sent to a portal is no longer spread in a block around it and travels whole; a big troop travels even if only part of it is over the portal's footprint
    Buried Explorador: it does not attack or counterattack on its own (only on an explicit attack order); it is drawn one tile lower, with the selection ellipse and the golden ellipse the same size and a dashed outline that follows its texture; click its texture to select it, and it leaves a furrow with pebbles when moving underground; Bury, moving and surfacing show in the resolution magnifier
    Several troops of the same type selected: the panel shows their common weapons and abilities, and the chosen weapon and the ability apply to all of them (the orders form a group)
    Hold Space: everything that is not your troops is shown at 35 %, and your troops get an outline that follows the shape of their texture (solid above ground, dashed underground); the left click does not select what is dimmed and the right click still gives orders. Selecting the idle troops moves to the I key

v2026.11.08 alpha
    Advanced tutorial: the five new lessons are now translated into all languages
    Buried troops: fixed cases where a troop buried or hidden under a big troop (Meca, APC, Raptor) could lose its place on the map when an allied big troop walked over it or moved aside to make way
    A key: arms the move with vigilance (the cursor becomes the attack sight and the left click queues the order; on an enemy it attacks); Ctrl+A replaces the earlier orders. The order's card shows a flag and an eye, and the troops stop to fire at whatever comes into range on the way
    WASD no longer pans the map (arrow keys only), because A is now the attack key
    Double click on a troop: selects all troops of the same type in sight on the screen; a group is only recalled from its card in the Groups island
    Turn resolution: if nothing else is happening on the screen, a lupa follows the troops marching far away so there are no «nothing happens» moments
    The lupas of tall buildings grow vertically instead of zooming out
    The default microturn is now 600 ms
    Tutorial: lesson 6 also teaches the double click, A, Ctrl+A and Ctrl+number

v2026.11.07 alpha
    Advanced tutorial: five new lessons (upgrades and Government level, Technology and Healing, loans and rationing, creatures and chests, expansions), each with its own practice match, from the «Tutorial» button on the start screen; the groups lesson also teaches how to save and recall selections
    Selection: Ctrl+1–0 saves the selected troops and 1–0 recalls them (numbers no longer pick groups, which are picked from the Groups island or by double click); Shift+click adds a troop to the selection and Ctrl+click removes it
    A friendly or allied building is drawn see-through when a troop stands on it, and the troop-building buttons grey out when no more fit in the order
    Chests: the Meca and other big troops can now open a chest or fight for it from an adjacent tile; Clonar walks up by itself before casting, and the preview of an out-of-range ability is faint instead of a cross
    The arrows of cascaded orders no longer blink when a troop belongs to several groups
    AI: builds more Raptors and repeats fewer orders; the lupas show the new states (burning, slowed, EMP…) when the hit lands

v2026.11.06 alpha
    Lupas: buildings finished close to each other share one lupa, order error notices appear once and last long enough to be read, lupas zoom in more, no longer cut an upgrade's animation and sit right above the event
    Lobbies with no connected player for 5 minutes close by themselves
    A buried troop never blocks the way of other troops, friendly or enemy
    First launch of the native client: the player name comes pre-filled (it can be changed)
    Meca: Shield+ (level 3) replaces Fuel-, with Stomp and Reflector shield retuned; Scientist: Healing and EMP Shock retuned
    Intermediate tutorial: five new lessons (troop groups, Shift and replacing orders, range and simultaneous combat, energy and defences, defending against an attack), each with its own practice match, from the «Tutorial» button on the start screen

v2026.11.04 alpha
    Basic tutorial: five short lessons, playing for real, from the "Tutorial" button on the start screen; each one starts a practice match against a passive AI, with yellow boxes that limit the orders to what each step teaches and a guided fight in lesson 4
    Welcome notice the first time the game is opened, recommending the tutorial
    Start screen: Settings, Tutorial and News open without dimming the background, and News becomes an icon next to the language selector

    Explorer: its legs move faster when walking
    Player icons: each player picks the face of a Terran unit (random the first time) on the start screen and in the lobby; the colour is now chosen on each lobby row, and the AI uses the field advisor's face

v2026.11.03 alpha
    Support: Healing heals 20% of the max health of any allied troop (living, machines and aircraft), EMP shock strips 50% of the max shield of enemy troops and buildings instead of all of it, and Paralyzing gas affects light and medium enemy ground troops, organic or machine (not heavy ones or aircraft)
    Droid: Clone copies every allied troop in the 3×3 (Constructors and large troops too) at 200 energy per troop copied, with a magenta circular preview, and the Portal carries large troops too; enemy decoys are revealed where an Explorer with "Detect hidden" or a Government detects
    AI: ignores detected enemy decoys and clones the most powerful group of troops
    Downloads: the Linux client is now a .tar.gz (it keeps the execute permission) and the website offers a one-line command with a copy button for Windows and Linux, which avoids the SmartScreen warning; the in-game updater understands the .tar.gz; the game also gets a Mac version (Apple Silicon and Intel), with its own command on the website and in-game updating

v2026.11.02 alpha
    Room chat: a column at the right of the room (up to 8 players) with the messages of everyone inside; the room now has three columns with players and map of the same width, the map is square and "Leave" and "Regenerate" are icon buttons
    Start screen chat: one channel for everybody connected that replaces the News panel (small text, scroll, the last 50 messages for whoever joins later and at most one message per second)
    The "News" button replaces the "History" one: it opens the changes of the latest version (with a "new" mark until you open it) and a button there leads to the full history
    Resolution: the lupas of simultaneous events open in parallel, look for the nearest free spot and are shared by the shots of one scene; the microturn is set in the "Game" menu and the workers panel is drawn from a cache (from 37 to 67 FPS in a heavy resolution)
    Web client: it opens the full start screen and room like the native client (up to 8 players, cooperative, passwords, saved matches and replays) and warns that performance is lower
    Web client: the first visit enters as "Web Player", with the page's language and a random colour
    Animated Milky Way background on the start, room, load game and replays screens: it fills the whole window (twinkling stars, breathing nebulae, a slow drift and the odd shooting star) and the panels are 80 % opaque; it can be switched off in Menu → Video ("Menus"; the Low preset turns it off)

v2026.11.01 alpha
    Shadows: buildings, troops, creatures, plants and aircraft cast a shadow with their own silhouette; the sun is chosen at the start of every day round (never below 45° and never pointing at you), there are no shadows at night and the weather softens and blurs them; aircraft fly higher and their shadow is detached from the ground
    Ground and liquids: plain ground no longer repeats its textures (rotations, tones and decorative plants) and water and oil have a moving texture that ripples with drops; maps now use 13 decoration pieces (9 that did not fit are removed)
    Maps: the Mecha and the APC are no longer trapped in their own area by passes that only met at a corner; they reach every area by at least one way
    AI: it uses the Bank in an emergency (one resource run out and another far in surplus, a trade only), builds its extractors in its own base before using an ally's coverage, and taking back "End turn" no longer waits for the other machine that plans its orders
    Interface: focusing a pool shows a glowing, transparent outline instead of the per-tile fill, and its tooltip is more transparent; water and oil sink the ground along their shore like a reservoir; bridges and ramps have a futuristic look of steel with lights; the workers panel blinks with a glow while workers are missing; and picking a troop or a building in the right panel centres the camera on it and a golden ellipse highlights it
    The right panel with nothing selected has two sections, each with its own scroll: your troops above and the buildings that produce troops below
    A "Video" menu with High, Medium and Low presets and 17 graphic effects you can switch on or off (weather, shadows, water and oil, fog, terrain, units, combat and buildings), and an optional FPS counter in Settings
    Rules: the Scientist's abilities (Healing, EMP shock, Paralyzing gas) and Clone reach 5 tiles, the Droid costs 250 energy (80 steel), and an order to a blocked tile (chest, obstacle) ends on the free tile beside it by the shortest route
    AI: it repairs its allies' damaged buildings
    Interface: troops show a portrait with an animated face in the selection panel and the sheet; the chat loses its tabs and gains an X button, and the ping button moves to the header (it also pings on the minimap) with bigger rings; a building animates production and research at once; and the lupa no longer shows a building vanish before the last shot it receives

v2026.10.31 alpha
    Abilities aimed out of range (Healing, Paralyzing gas, EMP shock, Mines): the troop walks up and casts in one order, and the zone stays highlighted until the round resolves it
    A faster resolution: the moves, shots and abilities of one microturn play together and the camera no longer waits; more events open a lupa (support abilities, mines, failed orders, building sites, discoveries, chest guards, chest prizes) and every notice floats up as text in your language
    Groups: Ctrl+right click moves a whole group in one request (no more flicker), the group's arrow is straight from the ellipse's centre to the destination, and arrows only show for the selected or pointed troop or group; cancelling a group order from one troop only takes that troop out of it
    New "Groups" panel next to the selection with the groups numbered 1-10 and keys 1…0 to pick them; one panel per building (ficha and orders) and per troop, with the detail inside it while Shift is held
    Maps: big troops (Meca, APC) reach everything small ones do (bridges and passes two cells wide), and a Constructor no longer stands still behind an allied troop when crossing its own building
    Windows: no more console window opening and closing; the fog and the ground no longer show blocks or lines
    A quick tutorial by default (12 short briefings, only what you need to play); the extended one is switched on in Settings or with a checkbox on every tutorial panel, and the Shift detail of a troop or building now fits its panel
    Fog of war as clouds: whatever you have not explored is covered by dense black storm clouds that drift slowly and now and then flash with lightning, and explored ground you cannot see is veiled by light clouds; graphics-card memory use also drops (mostly in resolutions with lupas), and the frame of a build tile half hidden by the scroll no longer covers the End Turn button

v2026.10.29 alpha
    New orders panel: the civil and military order columns are gone and the map fills the screen; when you select a troop, the panel at the bottom left of the map shows its whole queue in the order it actually runs (its own and its group orders mixed) and, with several troops, the orders they have in common
    Groups: double-clicking a troop selects its most recent group (the troops that share orders); "Belongs to group" lets you pick each group again with one click, clicking a troop of the selection keeps just that one, and group orders show in their group's color

v2026.10.28 alpha
    Allied AIs help each other with the economy: they ask their allies (humans or AIs) for resources when one is about to run out within 2 rounds, each ally accepts or declines according to its surplus and several can cover one request; a human sees a notice with Accept and Decline, and allies can see each other's resources
    Allied AIs defend each other: the reserve goes to an allied Government under attack (before its own expansions) and to other allied buildings (after them), each AI in proportion to its army; if its base is about to fall, the AI donates its resources and evacuates its troops to an ally
    Cooperative joint attack: the AI with the biggest army places a rally mark (Alt+click in the allies channel; Alt+Shift+click cancels it, and the AIs also follow a human's mark), the allied armies gather and attack the nearest enemy Government together with 1.3x superiority, supporting each other if attacked on the march; with less strength they raid the enemy economy. Also, an AI builds generators for an ally without power, helps a lagging ally upgrade its Government, calls for help in the chat when attacked, and the AIs split the exploration
    The AI attacks opportunistically: it counterattacks with its whole army after defending its base or an expansion, attacks weaker enemy armies it spots, attacks enemy expansions next to its own, and takes the windows when the rival army is far away to strike its Government
    The AI defends its expansions: it leaves Scouts on watch at the approaches, gathers its reserve at an equidistant point and sends it to the threatened expansion if it is stronger than the attacker; otherwise it keeps its troops and gives the expansion up
    A unit destroyed in the same instant no longer fires its queued attack order (a dead enemy could keep shooting before)
    Abilities no longer have a phase of their own: each one goes off at the start of the microturn in which it is ready (Healing, Paralyzing gas and EMP shock affect whoever is in their area at that moment) and whatever is queued after a troop's ability waits for it to go off
    The end of the game cuts right after the blow that destroys the Government: shots and counterattacks of the same microturn no longer count and the microturns stop as soon as there is a winner
    Towers last half as long: the Ground Defense drops to 400 HP and 100 shield (was 800 and 200) and the Air Defense to 300 and 75 (was 600 and 150)

v2026.10.27 alpha
    The AI manages its economy on demand, with no fixed limits: it builds extractors, farms, generators, Housing, Training Centers and Factories as it needs them, and trains as many units as it can feed (keeping a round of food in reserve)
    The AI uses the Bank only in emergencies (it used to take loans in almost every match); it orders the Government upgrade only when it can pay for it and builds another wood extractor when it runs short
    The AI buys technology with its spare Energy, no longer staffs unneeded generators, and spreads its Constructors over zones (the base and each expansion) so they walk less to distant sites
    Each pasture tile now gives 3,500 Food (it was 1,000)
    Hunted creatures stay visible until the hit that kills them, even if they were out of sight at the start of the round
    Maps: the iron mine next to each Government is symmetric between bases that mirror each other, and a free 3×3 lot for the Bank is always kept
    Maps: pasture patches are 12 tiles and the ones farther from the bases are bigger (up to 20) to encourage expanding; isolated pockets of land no longer appear
