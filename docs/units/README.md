# Units of the Terran Faction

<p><b>English</b> · <a href="../unidades/README.md">Español</a></p>

Sheets for the nine Terran units: production, upkeep, defense, movement and what each Government level unlocks (weapon, active ability and passive ability).

Values match **balance v11** and may change while the game is in development.

| | Unit | Class · cap · building | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/trabajador.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Worker**](trabajador.md) | Non-military · Civilian cap · Extractors and Generators | Civilian unit assigned to a resource extractor or an energy generator. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/constructor.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Constructor**](constructor.md) | Light Class · Civilian cap · Government level 1 | Civilian unit that builds the civilization's buildings. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/explorador.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Explorer**](explorador.md) | Light Class · Melee · Government level 1 | Light-class military unit with a melee attack effective against light enemies. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/militar.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Military**](militar.md) | Light Class · Military cap · Training Center | Light-class military unit trained for ranged combat. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/cientifico.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Scientist**](cientifico.md) | Light Class · No attack · Training Center | Light-class military support unit with abilities that help allies and hinder enemies. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/transporte_apc.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**APC Transport**](transporte_apc.md) | Medium Class · Military cap · Ground Factory | Medium-class military unit mainly in charge of transporting light troops. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/meca.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Mech**](meca.md) | Heavy Class · Military cap · Ground Factory | Heavy-class military unit trained for medium-range combat with area effect. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/raptor.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Raptor**](raptor.md) | Medium Air Class · Military cap · Launch Pad | Medium-class military air unit specialized in ground bombing and ground-to-air attack. |
| <img src="../../assets/img/units/droide.png" width="64" alt=""> | [**Droid**](droide.md) | Light Air Class · No attack · Launch Pad | Light-class military air unit with no attack, specialized in opening dimensional portals that teleport troops. |

All nine sheets laid out for printing, one A4 page per unit: [Unit-Sheets-Terran.pdf](Unit-Sheets-Terran.pdf).
